# Raised Garden Bed Blueprint & Layout Bundle

## Domain Vocabulary & Concepts
- **Square-Foot Gardening (SFG)**: Dividing planting areas into 1-foot square sections to maximize yield and eliminate wasted space.
- **Companion Planting**: Grouping mutually beneficial crops (e.g., tomatoes + basil + marigolds) to deter pests, optimize soil nutrients, and boost flavor, while isolating antagonists (e.g., fennel, potatoes near squash).
- **Modular Raised Bed**: Standardized dimensional wood configurations (4'x4', 4'x8', 2'x8' narrow border, L-shaped 8'x8' corner).
- **Lumber Cut List**: Precise breakdown of dimensional lumber (e.g. 2x6 cedar, 4x4 corner posts, 3" coated exterior deck screws).
- **Soil Volume Mix**: Mel's Mix or compost/peat/vermiculite calculation in cubic feet / cubic yards (e.g., 4'x8' at 12" depth = 32 cu ft = ~1.2 cu yds).
- **Succession Sowing**: Phased planting schedule for early spring cool crops, warm summer staples, and fall freeze-tolerant greens.
- **Conversion Seams**: Mobile announcement bar, hero action button, sticky purchase bar, and bottom conversion anchor pointing to Shopify checkout.
